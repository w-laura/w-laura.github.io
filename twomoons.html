Project Overview

TwoMoons is an "immersive" tcg board game bar located next to Stettbach station in Zurich. The space is designed as a crashed spaceship. It is a place where players from all walks of life gather to play, connect, and explore. My role as co-founder was to shape the user experience, narrative, and visual identity.

Narrative & Design Concept

The world of Aldria TwoMoons’ backstory unfolds on Aldria — a planet illuminated by two moons, Verdona and Luminara. After a small exploration vessel crash-lands, its crew transforms their broken ship into a gathering hub for travelers and locals alike. The idea of "community through play" became the emotional foundation for the space.

Discovering the setting From the beginning, we knew the theme would belong to the realm of science fiction, but the exact setting only revealed itself when I first entered the physical location. The irregular walls and light immediately evoked the image of a crashed spaceship in the woods.

Geometry of TwoMoons Instead of forcing the room into a conventional grid, I embraced its non-square geometry. In spaceships nothing is square, it's either rounded or hexagonal/octagonal. These hexagons and octagons then became the design identity of TwoMoons.
