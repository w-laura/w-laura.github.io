TwoMoons

An immersive board game bar — from concept to completion

Project Overview

Role: Co-Founder / Experience & Technical Lead · Location: Zurich · Year: 2023-present

TwoMoons is an immersive board game bar. I led the guest experience design, technical infrastructure, and the venue’s overall look & feel — combining narrative design, rapid prototyping, and a production build-out.

Story & Concept

Narrative seed. A cozy waystation orbiting two moons where travelers trade tales and games.

Experience pillars. Warmth, curiosity, and playful discovery — expressed through lighting, materials, and micro-interactions.

Flow. Entry → discovery wall → game selection → table experience → community moments.
